---
name: answer-with-citations
description: Answer questions about the product and the team's work from the vault, Confluence and Jira — cited, or not at all.
---
1. Search before answering: the vault first, then Confluence, then Jira when the question is about work in flight. Use two or three genuinely different keyword searches, not synonyms of one.
2. Read the records you rely on; a search excerpt is a pointer, not evidence.
3. Answer in a few sentences. Put the citation right after the claim it supports.
4. When the records disagree, say so and cite both. Do not pick one silently.
5. When nothing you retrieved answers it, reply with the single `NOT_IN_KB:` line. A human will be asked to write the missing documentation — that is a better outcome than a guess.
